What milling in Uttar Pradesh actually looks like

Uttar Pradesh has an enormous number of mills spread very thin, and most of them are family-run operations where the owner is also the buyer, the credit manager and the person who settles disputes. The constraint is not sophistication — it is that one person cannot personally watch a gate, a bridge and a godown at the same time, and the munshi's register is the only thing standing in for them.

Where Uttar Pradesh mills leak

  • The owner is the system — when he is at a mandi or a wedding, the record is whatever gets remembered later
  • Small mills cannot justify a Windows licence and an operator, so most of them run on paper and a phone
  • Supplier khata runs on long informal credit, and the balance everyone agrees on is the one nobody has written down

None of this shows up in Tally as a line, because there is no transaction to record — it is an absence. On the two to six percent margins a mill actually runs, that absence is the profit.

What MillSaathi does

One system that captures reality at the source — gate, weighbridge, lab, production — and reconciles every lot against it. It does not replace Tally. It feeds Tally numbers that are actually true.

  • Gate & Weighbridge — truck, driver and lot logged on entry; gross, tare and net captured once and never re-typed

In the language your mill floor speaks

The people entering data in Uttar Pradesh mostly work in Hindi. Hindi on the mill floor is on the roadmap alongside Hindi, Telugu and Tamil.
